QGIS + Sentinel-2 + SCP / Random Forest workflow

Islamabad LULC Analysis

A QGIS-based land cover classification workflow for Islamabad, combining training samples, classification, and index analysis to understand water extent and built-up growth in the city.

What the workflow covered

  • Sentinel-2 imagery
  • Training samples
  • Classification
  • NDVI
  • MNDWI
  • Built-up analysis
  • LULC mapping

Sentinel-2 imagery was classified in QGIS using the Semi-Automatic Classification Plugin / Random Forest workflow, with NDVI and MNDWI computed alongside the classification to support vegetation and water-extent interpretation, and a built-up analysis layered in to read urban growth patterns across the city.
